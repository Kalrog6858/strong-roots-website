What we offer

Everything we do starts with listening

Every service below is free, confidential, and available at any hour. You never need a referral, a diagnosis, or a reason that sounds serious enough.

Active listening

Space to say what's actually going on, at your own pace, to someone whose only job in that moment is to hear you properly.

Emotional support

For the days that are heavy without being an emergency — grief, loneliness, stress, burnout, family strain, or a week that simply got too big.

Crisis support

When you're in distress, we stay with you. We help you steady yourself, think through what's safe, and decide what happens next — together, not alone.

Resource navigation

Long forms, closed intake lists, waitlists that go nowhere. We help you work out which service actually fits, and how to get through the door.

Referrals

Connections to mental health, housing, healthcare, food, income, employment, legal, settlement and community services across Ontario.

Follow-up for up to 90 days

With your consent, we check back in to see whether the referral worked. If something fell through, we help you pick it back up.

How a call works

Four steps, at your pace

1

A person answers

A trained support specialist, not a menu. Share your name or stay anonymous — entirely your choice.

2

You talk, we listen

No script and no rush. You can pause, change your mind, or say you don't know where to start.

3

We work it out together

We help you find the right service and explain what to expect from it — or simply stay on the line if that's what today calls for.

4

We follow up

If you consent, we check in over the next 90 days so nothing quietly falls apart after the call ends.

How we work

The commitments behind every call

Confidential

What you share stays with us. You can call without giving your name, and you decide how much to say.

Culturally responsive

Your background is context, not an obstacle. You shouldn't have to explain your culture before you can explain your problem.

Non-judgemental

No lectures, no assumptions, no ranking whose problem is worse. You are met as you are.

Led by you

You set the pace and the direction. We offer options and information; the decisions stay yours.

Ready when you are

Free, confidential, and open every hour of every day.
