Why we exist

Too many people struggle in silence — not because help isn't out there, but because it never felt like it was built for them.

Our job is not simply to answer a phone call. It is to help someone find a path forward.

Read our story

People in African, Black and Caribbean communities run into the same walls again and again: long waits, cost, language, stigma, and services that don't understand their culture or their circumstances. Some have been misunderstood before and decided not to try again.

Strong Roots exists to be a different first phone call — one where you are heard without judgement, supported by someone who understands where you're coming from, and connected to the help you actually need.

And while our work is centred on African, Black and Caribbean communities, our lines are open to anyone seeking guidance, connection and hope.

What we do

Support that meets you where you are

Whatever brought you to the phone, you can start anywhere. You don't need to have the right words ready.

Someone who listens

A trained support specialist gives you their full attention — no rushing, no script, no judgement. Sometimes being properly heard is the thing that changes the day.

Crisis support

When things feel like too much, we stay on the line with you, help you steady yourself, and work through what needs to happen next — one step at a time.

Finding the right service

Mental health, housing, food, income, legal, newcomer and settlement supports — we know the landscape and help you find the door that will actually open.

Warm referrals

We don't just hand you a phone number. We explain what a service does, what to expect, and how to reach it, so you're not starting from zero all over again.

Follow-up for up to 90 days

With your consent, we check back in. Did the referral work? Did something fall through? Staying connected is how people get past the barriers instead of giving up at them.

Support for families

Parents, caregivers, siblings and partners are welcome to call too. Helping someone you love is heavy work, and you deserve support while you do it.

What happens when you call

No forms. No waiting room. Just a conversation.

If you've never called a helpline before, here's exactly what to expect.

1

A person answers

Not a menu, not a machine. You'll be greeted by a trained support specialist. You can give your name or not — that's your choice.

2

You talk, we listen

Say as much or as little as you want. There's no test to pass and no situation too small to call about.

3

We work it out together

If you need a service, we help you find it and explain how to get there. If you just needed to talk, that's a complete call too.

4

We stay in touch

If you'd like, we can follow up for up to 90 days to make sure you got connected and nothing fell through the cracks.

A word from our founder

"I kept meeting people who had already given up on asking for help."

"They weren't difficult people, and they weren't beyond helping. They had reached out once, felt misunderstood, and decided it wasn't worth going through that again. That is a loss we can prevent.

Strong Roots started with a simple idea: what if the first phone call was a good one? What if the person on the other end understood your background without you having to explain it, and stayed with you long enough to actually get you somewhere?

We are building the line I wish had existed for people I know. Somewhere you can call at three in the morning and be met with patience instead of paperwork. Somewhere your culture is context, not a complication.

If you are holding something heavy right now, please call us. You are not a burden, and you are not alone."

Rufael
Founder & Chief Executive Officer, Strong Roots Crisis Helpline
